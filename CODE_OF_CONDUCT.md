# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that make this a good place to work:

- Being kind and assuming good faith
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it

Things that are not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and whenever someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair corrective action in
response to behaviour they consider inappropriate, up to and including a permanent ban. Report a concern
by opening a private security advisory or contacting a maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed
and handled discreetly.
